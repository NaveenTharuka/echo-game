# ECHO — Audio credits

trimmed, sliced into single hits, 2 ms fade-in / short fade-out, peak-normalised, 16-bit, ≤ 48 kHz,
loops crossfaded to be seamless. The originals are kept outside the project in `D:\Game Sounds and Models`.
